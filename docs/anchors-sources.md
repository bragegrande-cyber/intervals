## Corrections to the old data
- Star Wars: was "first two notes"; the fifth is between the two long notes after the opening triplet.
- The Entertainer: was "first two notes" (that is a minor second); the minor sixth is the jump after the pickup run.
- Love Story: was listed as minor sixth UP on the first two notes; the first two notes go DOWN (C→E). The upward sixth is notes 3–4.
- Obscure anchors ranked last: My Bonnie and NBC chimes (M6 up), Swing Low (M3 down), Nobody Knows (M6 down), Willow Weep (P8 down). Watermelon Man and I Love You stay as the only options for m7 down and M7 down.
